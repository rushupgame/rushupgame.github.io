Rush'Up! - Free multiplayer party & guessing game

Think fast, speak faster!

Play around the same table or across the world! Choose your preferred game mode and guess the same deck of cards across 3 intense rounds:

The 3 game rounds

💬 Round 1: free description

Describe the card on screen speaking freely without saying the word itself. Teammates can guess as many times as they want before the timer runs out!

☝️ Round 2: single word clue

The exact same deck returns! Give only one clue word per card. Be careful, your team gets only one guess per turn!

🎭 Round 3: mime & acting

Absolute silence! Use body language, acting, mimes, and sound effects to help your team guess the final cards in the deck.

⚡ Play local game (100% free)

📱 Local party game (in person)

One single phone for the whole room. Pass the smartphone around the table as your turn comes. Ideal for game nights, family dinners, or coffee breaks!

🎥 Play online video room

🎥 Online video room (remote play)

Play remotely with live video. Create a private room, share the code, see everyone’s camera in real-time, and let the app handle synced timers and card decks!

⚙️ The ultimate digital party game

Looking for the perfect card guessing game to spice up a birthday party, family dinner, or game night with friends? Rush'Up! brings all the excitement of classic party guessing games straight to your browser.

📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)

Add the app directly to your iOS or Android home screen with one tap. Enjoy smooth, instant gameplay—even completely offline.

📚 Over 25 fun themes

Content for everyone: pop culture, movies, anime, TV shows, gaming, history, geography, and kids packs for ages 4+.

🏆 Score tracker & leaderboards

Track individual player stats, count total points, generate automatic leaderboards, and award fun trophies at the end of the game.

🔒 100% free & no ads

Enjoy your fun party moments with zero ad interruptions, no hidden microtransactions, and complete respect for your data privacy.

💡 The iconic 3-round card guessing experience, now on mobile

If you love speed-based party games involving mimes, single clues, and a ticking clock, Rush'Up! captures the best mechanics of classic party card games. Forget carrying heavy game boxes, shuffling physical card decks, or hunting for a missing hourglass timer: everything is handled automatically on a single smartphone!

🎯 Our mission: bringing people together every day

In an era where technology often isolates individuals behind personal screens, our fundamental mission with Rush'Up! is to do the exact opposite: use digital innovation to create real-world human connections.

We strongly believe that shared party card games are the most powerful tool for breaking the ice, sparking laughter, and building lasting memories.

📖 The story behind Rush'Up!

The Rush'Up! project was born out of a simple frustration: wanting to play a great party guessing game with friends, but being stopped by missing physical equipment.

Created independently, this free web game was designed to deliver a fast, seamless, and universal party experience.
