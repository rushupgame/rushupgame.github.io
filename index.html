Rush'Up! - Free multiplayer party & guessing game

Think fast, speak faster!

Play around the same table or across the world! Choose your preferred game mode and guess the same deck of cards across 3 intense rounds:

The 3 game rounds

💬 Round 1: free description

Describe the card on screen speaking freely without saying the word itself. Teammates can guess as many times as they want before the timer runs out!

☝️ Round 2: single word clue

The exact same deck returns! Give only one clue word per card. Be careful, your team gets only one guess per turn!

🎭 Round 3: mime & acting

Absolute silence! Use body language, acting, mimes, and sound effects to help your team guess the final cards in the deck.

📋 How to play Rush'Up!: complete rules

Setting up a game of Rush'Up! takes less than a minute. Split your guests into two teams (or more, if you're hosting a big party), pick a card theme, and set the round timer — 60 seconds is the classic pace, but you can go faster for an even more chaotic party game, or slower for younger players and beginners.

Each round reuses the exact same deck of cards, which is what makes the game so replayable and so rewarding: the words you struggled to describe in round one become easy layups by round three, once your team has already heard them mimed or clued once before. That repetition is the secret behind why this style of party game keeps tables laughing for hours instead of minutes.

🧮 Scoring

Every correctly guessed card earns one point for the active team. Skipped cards go back into the deck and come up again before the timer ends, so no card is ever wasted.

👥 Team setup

Works great from 4 players upward. Larger groups can form 3 or 4 teams and rotate turns, making it a perfect party game for big family gatherings or office events.

⏱️ Timer & pace

The built-in timer is fully adjustable. Shorten it for a fast-paced, high-energy party game, or extend it for a more relaxed pace with kids or first-time players.

🚫 House rules

No rhyming words, no spelling the answer out loud, and no using the title itself in Rounds 1 and 2. Rush'Up! displays the rules on-screen so nobody forgets mid-party.

❓ Frequently asked questions

What is Rush'Up! and how do you play it?

Rush'Up! is a free browser-based party game where players guess words from a shared deck of cards across three rounds: free description, one-word clue, and mime. Play locally by passing one phone around the table, or remotely in a live video room.

Do I need to download an app to play?

No download is required. Rush'Up! runs directly in your browser as a Progressive Web App, with the option to add it to your home screen for offline play.

Is Rush'Up! really free?

Yes, 100% free with no ads and no in-app purchases. The project relies on optional donations from players who want to support it.

How many players do I need?

Rush'Up! works best with 4 or more players split into two teams, though the online room mode makes it easy to include remote friends from anywhere.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes — a dedicated kids pack is available for players aged 4 and up, alongside 25+ themes for teens and adults.
