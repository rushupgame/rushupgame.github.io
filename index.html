Rush'Up!

Think fast, speak faster!

A single phone is all you need to play. Form 2 to 5 teams and try to guess the same deck of cards across 3 intense rounds against the digital timer:

💬 Round 1: Free Description

Describe the card on screen speaking freely without saying the word itself. Teammates can guess as many times as they want before the timer runs out!

☝️ Round 2: Single Word

The exact same deck returns! Give only one clue word per card. Be careful, your team gets only one guess per turn!

🎭 Round 3: Mime & Sounds

Absolute silence! Use body language, acting, mimes, and sound effects to help your team guess the final cards in the deck.

🎯 Our Mission: Bringing People Together Every Day

In an era where technology often isolates individuals behind their personal screens, our fundamental mission with Rush'Up! is to do the exact opposite: use digital innovation to create real-world human connections.

We strongly believe that shared party games are the most powerful tool for breaking the ice, sparking laughter, and building lasting memories. Whether it's a family gathering, a birthday, a night out with friends, a coffee break with colleagues, or a road trip, Rush'Up! turns any moment into a joyful experience for all ages.

📖 The Story of Rush'Up!

The Rush'Up! project was born out of a simple frustration: wanting to play a great party guessing game with friends, but being stopped by missing equipment (bulky boxes to carry, broken timers, missing or worn-out cards).

Created independently, this free web platform was designed to deliver a fast, seamless, and universal party experience. Thanks to community feedback, the game is regularly updated with fresh theme packs and powerful new features.
